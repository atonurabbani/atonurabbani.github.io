Welcome

I am an economist at the Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies (BIDS), where I am Research Director of the General Economics Division, and a Professor of Economics at the University of Dhaka (currently on leave).

My research is in applied microeconomics, with work on health, firms and organizations, energy and the environment, and behaviour. Much of it uses field experiments and household or firm surveys to evaluate programs in Bangladesh, especially in the garment sector.

I hold a PhD (2007) and MA (2003) in Economics from the University of Chicago, and an MSS (2000) and BSS (Honors, 1998) in Economics from the University of Dhaka.

Areas of interest

Applied microeconomics · Health economics · Organizational economics · Environmental economics · Behavioral economics · Impact evaluation
